Room details, directions and policies

The new website now shows a description under every room, a map, and a policies panel. We filled them in with our best estimates so you could see the pages complete. Please read them as drafts and correct anything that is wrong.

For FTCI · 14 August 2026

Why we are asking. The figures below came from the design mock-up, not from FTCI's own records. They are already on the website, where a guest reads them as facts about your hotels. Every one of them needs a yes or a correction from someone who knows the property.

Nothing here changes a price. All rates are exactly as FTCI supplied them, and they are shown only so you can tell the rooms apart.

The site goes live on 20 August 2026.

How to answer

Write the right figure in the shaded Correction column — on screen, or print this page and write on it and send a photograph. Anything you leave blank, we take as correct. Where we have guessed something FTCI would rather not state at all, say "remove" and it comes off the page.

1 · Room details

For each room type: how many and what kind of beds, the maximum number of guests, the view, and the floor area if known.

The maximum-guests number is the most important one on this page. It is the answer to the question we have had open for a while about the two-bedroom units. Once a guest can book online, this number is what tells them whether the room fits their family — and it is the one figure a wrong guess turns into an argument at the front desk.

2 · How to reach

Each hotel page now shows a map, and the nearest airport and railway station.

The map pin marks the locality, not the building. We placed each one from the postcode or road named in the hotel's own address, so it lands a guest in the right town or settlement — but not at the gate.

If someone at each property sends us the exact spot — a Google Maps pin or a what3words, whichever is easier — we will move it.

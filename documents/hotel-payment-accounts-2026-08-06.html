One payment account per hotel

A step-by-step checklist for giving each hotel its own Razorpay account and its own bank settlement — while FTCI remains a single society under one PAN.

For FTCI · prepared 6 August 2026 · every link verified

🎯What this achieves

Each of the four hotels gets its own Razorpay account, settling into its own bank account. A guest booking a room at Gangtok pays into Gangtok's account, and a guest booking at McLeod Ganj pays into McLeod Ganj's — automatically, with no transfers between units and no manual reconciliation at the end of the month. FTCI stays one legal entity throughout; nothing about the society's registration changes.

None of the four is finished yet. Bylakuppe is furthest along — it is part-way through verification, and Razorpay have asked for a video KYC before the account can be used. Until that is complete and the dashboard opens, we treat Bylakuppe as in progress like the rest.

✅Step 1 — Two things to check first

Both of these are quicker to sort out now than to discover halfway through.

FTCI you do this Us the website team Both together
  • FTCI Does each hotel already have its own current account, in FTCI's name? If any hotel does not, opening it is the slowest step in the whole process — please start that with the bank straight away, ahead of anything on Razorpay's side. The account holder name must match the entity PAN exactly; a mismatch is the single most common cause of rejection.
  • FTCI Finish the verification already in progress. Razorpay have asked for a video KYC on the first account. Until that is done the account cannot be used and the dashboard will not open, so this comes before opening any further accounts. The person named as the authorised signatory is normally the one who must appear.
  • FTCI Confirm the mobile number can be reused. Razorpay lets you open further accounts using the same mobile number and email as the first one. If that number is already attached to several accounts under different email addresses, this option is blocked — worth checking before you begin.

💬Step 2 — One question to put to Razorpay before opening anything

Please raise this from inside the Razorpay dashboard rather than by email — a request raised while logged in is attached to your account, so their team can see your actual file.

Copy and send this We are a Multi-State Cooperative Society with one entity PAN, operating four hotels in four different states, each with its own GSTIN and its own current account. We need settlements to go to each hotel's own bank account. Can we open four separate merchant accounts under the same PAN, distinguished by GSTIN — and if so, what does each additional account require? We are not asking about Route.
Why we expect this to work. GST issues a separate registration number per state under a single PAN, and Razorpay allows one account per tax registration. Your hotels are in four different states, so each already has its own GSTIN. We would like Razorpay to confirm this in writing before you file anything, so that no time is spent on the wrong route.

⚠️Step 3 — One thing to decline

If Razorpay suggest “Route” or “Linked Accounts”, please do not agree to it on the call. Say you will come back to them, and tell us.

Route is built for splitting a payment out to third parties who serve your customers — for example a marketplace paying independent sellers. Your four hotels are all FTCI, so that is not what is happening here. Route also now requires a minimum turnover, and an application that is refused can remove the facility from the account altogether. Separate accounts per hotel are simpler, and give each property direct control of its own settlements.

📄Step 4 — For each hotel

The same set of documents for every property, all under the one entity PAN. Each account is reviewed separately, and each review typically takes 3–5 working days — plus a video KYC if Razorpay ask for one.

  • FTCI FTCI's entity PAN The same PAN for all four hotels — this does not change.
  • FTCI That hotel's GSTIN The state registration for the state the hotel operates in.
  • FTCI That hotel's own current account In FTCI's name, with the name matching the PAN exactly. Razorpay verifies it with a small test deposit.
  • FTCI Address proof for that property
  • FTCI Board resolution or authorisation letter Naming the office-bearer who will operate that unit's account.
  • FTCI Registration certificate and bye-laws The society's documents, submitted again for each account.

🔒Step 5 — When an account is approved

  • FTCI Tell us the account is live.
  • Both Add us as an Admin team member — not Owner. Admin lets us build and connect the website. It does not let us change ownership or alter your bank details. FTCI keeps full control of the account throughout.
  • Both Send us the technical keys when we ask for them. We will only need these at the point we connect that hotel. Send them whichever way is easiest for you — WhatsApp is fine. Two small things: please send them only when we ask, not in advance, and tell us once you have. As soon as the website is connected we will replace that key with a fresh one, so the key you messaged stops working. Nothing to do at your end — we handle the change and confirm when it is done.
Why we swap the key afterwards. A key sent by message stays in the chat history on both phones. Replacing it once the site is connected takes us a minute and means the copy in the chat is no longer of any use to anyone. It is a routine step, not a sign anything has gone wrong.

If it is a separate legal entity with its own PAN, it needs an ordinary new signup rather than an additional account under FTCI — different paperwork, and worth knowing before you start.

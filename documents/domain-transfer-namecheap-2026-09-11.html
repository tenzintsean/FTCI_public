Moving ftcihotels.com into your own account

A step-by-step guide. About 15 minutes and one card payment, then about a week of waiting while it completes on its own. Please start it today. The reason is explained just below.

For Tsetan

What this is. Webnock has sent us the “Domain Secret” for ftcihotels.com. It is a transfer password: it works like the porting code you get when you move a mobile number to a new network. Entering it at Namecheap, a well-known domain company, moves ftcihotels.com out of Webnock's account and into yours.

Why Namecheap and not Cloudflare? Cloudflare only accepts a transfer after a technical change that only Webnock can make. Namecheap accepts it as things stand today, so nothing more is needed from Webnock. The domain can move into Cloudflare, next to ftcihotels.org, later on.

Why today. The code has a limited life, like an OTP. Once it expires we would have to go back to Webnock for a new one.

Before you start

Have these to hand:

  • The Domain Secret from Webnock's message. When you copy it, take every character exactly, including the symbol at the very start, and no spaces before or after.
  • A card that works for international online payments, or a PayPal account. Expect about ₹1,100. Namecheap showed ₹1,096 on 11 September, plus a small registry fee of US $0.20. This also pays for one more year of the domain.
  • Your phone, for the bank's OTP.
  • The same Gmail address you use for Cloudflare. It will be your Namecheap login.
Treat the Domain Secret like a password. Anyone who has it can try to move the domain to themselves. Please don't forward it, post it in a group chat, or paste it anywhere except the Namecheap page below.

The steps

  1. Open Namecheap's transfer page

    Go to this address. You don't need an account yet:

    https://www.namecheap.com/domains/transfer/

    If you land on Namecheap's home page instead, click Transfer to Us in the top menu.

  2. Enter the domain name

    In the box labelled Enter the domain name you want to transfer, type exactly:

    ftcihotels.com

    Then click the Transfer button next to it.

  3. Check that it says “eligible” and “unlocked”

    A page headed Transfer Eligibility opens. It should say:

    ✅ Congratulations! Your domain ftcihotels.com is eligible for transfer.

    Under Transfer checklist, the Registrar Lock line should say Domain is UNLOCKED. We checked both on 11 September.

    ⚠️ Stop here if it says anything else

    For example, not eligible or locked. Don't try another website. Send us a screenshot, and we'll sort it out with Webnock.

  4. Check the code before paying anything

    This is the most important step, and it costs nothing.

    If there is a checkbox that says By requesting my domain to be transferred, I agree for Namecheap to retrieve my domain's contact information., tick it.

    Paste the Domain Secret into the box labelled Authorization Code, then click Verify Auth Code. Namecheap will tell you whether the code is accepted.

    ⚠️ If Namecheap says the code is not valid, stop

    Check first that you copied the whole code, including the symbol at the start. If it is still refused, don't pay. The code has most likely expired.

    Message us, and we'll ask Webnock for a fresh one. Nothing has been bought at this point, so nothing is lost.

  5. Add it to the cart

    Click Add to cart, then View Cart.

  6. Check the cart and add nothing else

    The cart should contain only the ftcihotels.com transfer, for 1 year.

    • Withheld for Privacy is free. Leave it on.
    • PremiumDNS: leave it off.
    • No hosting, email, SSL certificate or any other extra. Namecheap will offer several, and we need none of them.

    If there is an auto-renew switch, leave it as it is. Indian cards can't auto-renew at Namecheap anyway, and we will take care of the renewal well before December 2027.

    Then click Confirm Order.

  7. Create your Namecheap account

    Namecheap asks you to sign up at this point. Fill in:

    • Your first and last name.
    • The Gmail address you use for Cloudflare.
    • A username: letters and numbers only. It can't be changed later, so please write it down.
    • A strong password that you keep safe.

    If Namecheap sends a confirmation email, open it and confirm.

  8. Pay

    Pay by card or PayPal and approve the bank OTP.

    If the card is declined: open your bank's app, turn on international / online transactions for the card, and try once more. PayPal is the other option.

    ⚠️ If payment still won't go through, stop

    Don't try other websites on your own. Message us, because there is a backup that charges in rupees.

  9. Turn on two-step login

    Whoever can log into this Namecheap account controls ftcihotels.com, so it deserves the same protection as your bank. In Namecheap, go to:

    Profile → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Manage

    Choose an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator, and follow the prompts.

After you pay: about a week of waiting

You don't need to do anything during the wait. Webnock's registrar may email Webnock about the transfer. If nobody there replies, the transfer goes through on its own after about 5 days. The old website keeps working exactly as it is during the wait.

To watch progress: Namecheap → Domain List → Filters → Pending Transfer. You may see these, in this order:

What Namecheap showsWhat it means
Transfer will begin shortlyAll good. Nothing to do.
Awaiting release from previous registrarAll good. This is the ~5-day wait.
ftcihotels.com appears in your normal Domain ListDone. Tell us.
Awaiting authorization codeNamecheap wants the code again. See below.

If it says Awaiting authorization code: go to Domain List, find ftcihotels.com, click Authorize Transfer, paste the same Domain Secret, then Save Changes.

If Namecheap emails that the transfer was cancelled, for example because of an invalid code, message us. The money goes back to your Namecheap balance automatically, and we'll resubmit with a fresh code.

Please don't

  • Change the nameservers or any DNS setting for ftcihotels.com at Namecheap. We will do that together in the next step, with everything already prepared.
  • Buy anything else Namecheap offers.
  • Share the Domain Secret or your Namecheap password with anyone.

When it's done

Send us a message saying “ftcihotels.com is in my Namecheap account”, with a screenshot of your Domain List. Please include your Namecheap username, but never the password.

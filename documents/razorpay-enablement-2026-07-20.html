Enabling Online Payments for FTCI

How we switch on Razorpay so the new website can take hotel booking payments and donations directly — keeping the revenue with the cooperatives instead of paying 15–25% to travel aggregators.

For FTCI review · prepared 20 July 2026 · every link verified

🎯Why this matters

Today, hotel bookings come through Agoda and MakeMyTrip, who take a 15–25% commission on every booking. Our own website + Razorpay lets guests pay FTCI directly. The only cost that remains is Razorpay's payment-processing fee of about 2% + GST — and even that is zero when a guest chooses “pay at hotel.” Razorpay is the right choice because it is India's standard gateway (UPI, cards, netbanking) and, unlike Stripe, fully supports Indian organizations receiving Indian payments.

Hotel bookings

Guests pay online (UPI/card) or choose pay-at-hotel — instant confirmation, our own booking calendar.

Donations

FTCI stays in control

📋What FTCI needs to gather (the important part)

Because FTCI is a registered cooperative society, Razorpay uses its “Trust / Society” verification path. Please have these ready — gathering them is the single biggest thing that determines how fast we go live:

  • Entity PAN card — FTCI's own PAN (not an individual's).
  • Registration certificate — the Multi-State Cooperative Societies registration certificate (Registrar of Cooperative Societies).
  • Bye-laws / constitutional document — the society's bye-laws (equivalent of a trust deed / MOA-AOA). confirm exact format at signup
  • Board resolution / authorization letter naming the authorized signatory who will operate the Razorpay account and the linked bank account.
  • Cancelled cheque or bank statement in FTCI's name — a current account (not savings/personal). The name must exactly match the entity PAN.
  • Authorized signatory's personal PAN.
  • Authorized signatory's address proof — Aadhaar / passport / voter ID / driving licence.
  • Registered address proof — utility bill or similar, if asked separately.
  • (Only for donations) FTCI's 80G certificate and 12A registration — needed only if you want automatic tax-exemption donation receipts. check eligibility with your CA
#1 cause of delay: a name mismatch across PAN, bank account, and registration certificate. Please make sure the entity name reads identically on all three before we submit.

🪜The enablement steps, and who does each

FTCI (you) — the account owner does this Us — the development team does this Both — done together
  1. Sign up for the Razorpay account. FTCI An authorized officer registers at razorpay.com with FTCI's official email and phone. The account must belong to FTCI, not to us.
  2. Choose the business type. FTCI Select “Registered business” → Society / Trust in the onboarding form.
  3. Upload the KYC documents. FTCI Submit the documents from the list above. Only FTCI can do this — Razorpay does not allow us to submit KYC on your behalf. See the official document checklist.
  4. Link the settlement bank account. FTCI Provide FTCI's current-account details; Razorpay verifies with a small test deposit. This is where all booking and donation money will land.
  5. Razorpay reviews & activates. FTCI Razorpay's compliance team reviews — typically 3–5 working days for a society/trust. We don't wait idle: see the timeline below.
  6. Give us developer access — safely. Both FTCI invites us as a limited “Admin” team member (not Owner), and generates the API keys, sharing the secret only via a password manager — never over chat or email. Details in the Security section.
  7. We wire it into the website. Us We integrate Razorpay Checkout, build the booking/donation flows and refunds, and set up the webhook that confirms each payment. We build and test this in Test Mode in parallel with your KYC.
  8. Go live & verify. Both Once activated, we switch to live keys, run one small real transaction end-to-end to confirm the money settles, and hand you a short runbook. FTCI keeps full dashboard access throughout.

⏱️Timeline — FTCI's sign-up is the critical path

Razorpay activation for a society/trust takes ~3–5 working days — longer if any document name doesn't match. This review is the single longest lead-time item in the entire project, and it sits entirely on FTCI's side. We are already building and testing the payment system in parallel (in Test Mode), so we will be ready and waiting — but we cannot switch on live payments until FTCI's KYC is approved.

Put plainly: every day the sign-up is delayed is a day taken off the 20 August launch. If the account is not approved in time, the site opens without working online payments — and FTCI keeps paying aggregator commission in the meantime. Please begin the sign-up and gather the documents this week — it is the one thing only FTCI can do, and the one thing that can hold up the opening.

🙏Donations & 80G tax receipts

If FTCI holds 80G / 12A registration, Razorpay can automatically issue tax-exemption receipts to donors — a strong trust signal that increases giving. This is a checkbox on a Payment Page once your 80G details are added. how 80G receipts work · Razorpay for NGOs

A hotel-operating cooperative may or may not qualify for 80G on all inflows. Please have FTCI's CA confirm eligibility before we advertise “80G receipt” to donors.

🔒Keeping FTCI in control & secure

FTCI owns the account

The Owner login, bank account and API keys all belong to FTCI. We are added only as an “Admin” team member — we can build, but we cannot change ownership or bank details.

API keys shared safely

You generate the Key ID + Secret; the Secret is shown only once. Share it through a password manager, never chat or email. We store it server-side, never in the website's public code.

Every payment double-checked

We confirm a booking only after Razorpay's signed webhook verifies the payment — not just an on-screen “success” — so no booking is confirmed without money actually received.

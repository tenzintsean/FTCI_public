A cooperative welcome, since 2005

The Federation of Tibetan Cooperatives in India Ltd. was established on 20 April 2005, registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Society Act. It unites 15 Tibetan cooperative societies across seven states and functions democratically under the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies.

From its Bengaluru head office, FTCI runs four hospitality units and supports over 23,000 shareholders — striving to strengthen the social and economic sustainability of Tibetan settlements. When you stay with us, your visit helps sustain that community.
